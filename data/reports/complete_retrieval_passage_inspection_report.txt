Purpose

This report combines the Dense, BM25, and Hybrid retrieval evaluations.
It inspects the actual chunk text behind every top-3 result for all 10
evaluation questions: 30 results per method, 90 retrieved passages total.

Evaluation setup
----------------
Dense: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 with cosine similarity.
BM25: lexical keyword retrieval.
Hybrid: Dense + BM25 combined with Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF), candidate_k=10, RRF k=60.
Evaluation metric: Hit@3, where a hit means the expected source paper appears in the top 3.

passage relevance, groundedness, citation correctness, or whether rank 1 is the
best supporting passage. The passage text is therefore inspected separately.
